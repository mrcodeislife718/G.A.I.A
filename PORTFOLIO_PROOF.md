# G.A.I.A — Portfolio Proof Contract

**Track:** Research / causal intelligence

G.A.I.A is complete only when causal claims outperform correlation-only or naive baselines on controlled and unfamiliar problems, with uncertainty and intervention evidence preserved.

Required proof: explicit causal representations and inference paths; tests for confounding, temporal order, interventions, counterfactuals, missing variables, and contradictory evidence; adversarial causal traps; benchmarks on synthetic ground-truth and real datasets where appropriate; calibration and error decomposition; reproducible experiments and limitations.

**Next proof target:** build a suite with known causal graphs plus hidden confounders and distribution shifts, compare G.A.I.A against correlation-only baselines, and score graph recovery, intervention prediction, counterfactual accuracy, and calibration.
